Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

This policy explains how LUNATIKS Inc. ("LUNATIKS", "we") handles personal data when you use this website and when we work on a client's records, including records in QuickBooks Online through our Bookkeeper app. Contact: lunatiksinc@gmail.com.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. Our host keeps standard server logs, such as the address a request came from and the page asked for, to run and protect the site.

Client records

When a business engages us, we handle its records to do the work it has engaged us for. Those records can include personal data about the business's owners, staff, customers and suppliers, for example names, contact details, amounts paid and bank details shown on invoices or statements.

  • QuickBooks data. With the client's written authority, Bookkeeper reads the client's QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's connection, and writes to it only as the client has approved. We use QuickBooks data only to do the client's work. We do not sell it, use it for marketing or use it to train AI models.
  • Documents. Documents a client sends us, such as invoices and receipts, are stored for the client's work. Our software may send a document to an AI model provided by Anthropic to read its contents; a named person then checks what it read before anything is used.
  • Separation. Each client's records are kept apart from every other client's.

Why we may handle it

We handle client records to perform our engagement with the client and to meet our own legal obligations, including record keeping and checks on who our clients are. Where a client's records include personal data, the client remains responsible for having collected it lawfully. For the records we work on for a client, we act on the client's instructions under our engagement. For the checks we must make on who our clients are, and for our own business records, we decide how the data is used and are responsible for it.

Who else receives it

  • Intuit, as the provider of QuickBooks Online, under the client's own agreement with Intuit.
  • Anthropic, for reading documents as described above.
  • FXVPS, which runs the server where client records are kept, in the United Kingdom.
  • Authorities, where the law requires us to share it.

Client records are kept on our server in the United Kingdom. Intuit and Anthropic may handle data in other countries, including the United States, under their own terms and the safeguards those terms provide. Ask us for details.

How we protect it

  • Keys for a client's QuickBooks company are encrypted, readable only by the account that does that client's work and tied to that one company.
  • QuickBooks data, documents and keys are never written to our logs.
  • Access to client records is limited to the people doing the client's work.

How long we keep it

We keep client records for six years after the end of the period they relate to, or longer where the law or a dispute requires it, then delete them. Records of the checks we make on who our clients are we keep for five years after the client relationship ends, then delete them. When a QuickBooks connection ends we delete its key.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how it is used and to complain to a data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom or the Data Protection Commissioner in Barbados. If your data reached us through a business that is our client, we may pass your request to that business. To ask, contact lunatiksinc@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for a material change, tell our clients first.
