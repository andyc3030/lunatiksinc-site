Connect or reconnect your QuickBooks company

Bookkeeper is connected by the LUNATIKS team, not from this page, so that each connection matches a client's written engagement and the company on our record.

If you are a client and want to connect, or your connection has ended and needs to be renewed, contact us at lunatiksinc@gmail.com. We will arrange a short session in which a person authorised for your company signs in to Intuit and chooses the company. Intuit asks a company to renew the connection from time to time; we will tell you before that is needed.

If you are not a LUNATIKS client, there is nothing to connect. How Bookkeeper works.
