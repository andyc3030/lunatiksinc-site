Bookkeeper and your QuickBooks company

Bookkeeper is the software LUNATIKS uses to prepare bookkeeping work for its clients. It is not sold to the public and there is nothing to install. Once your company is connected, the LUNATIKS team works on it for you; you do not need to sign in here.

How a company is connected

We connect a QuickBooks company only after the business has engaged LUNATIKS in writing. A person authorised for the company signs in to Intuit and chooses the company. Intuit then gives our software a key for that one company, and our software refuses any other company.

What Bookkeeper does with the connection

  • It reads the company's records, such as accounts, transactions, reports and tax codes, to prepare the work agreed with the client.
  • For a new client it only reads. It prepares entries for comparison with the client's own books and writes nothing to QuickBooks.
  • It writes to QuickBooks only after the client has approved that in writing, and then only entries a named person has reviewed and approved. Every entry it writes is read back and checked.
  • It keeps each client's records separate from every other client's.

How the connection is protected

  • The key Intuit issues is stored encrypted on our server, readable only by the account that runs the client's work and tied to that one company.
  • QuickBooks data and keys are never written to our logs.
  • We never sell QuickBooks data and use it only to do the work the client has engaged us for.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time in QuickBooks (Settings, then Apps) or by asking us. When the connection ends we delete the key and stop reading the company. What happens after you disconnect.
