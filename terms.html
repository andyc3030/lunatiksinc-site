Terms of use for Bookkeeper

Last updated: 27 November 2026

These terms apply when a business connects its QuickBooks Online company to Bookkeeper, the software LUNATIKS Inc. ("LUNATIKS", "we") uses to prepare work for its clients. They sit alongside the written engagement between LUNATIKS and the business; where the two differ, the engagement applies.

1. Who may connect

Only a business that has engaged LUNATIKS in writing may connect, and only a person authorised to act for that business may sign in and choose its company. By connecting you confirm you have that authority.

2. What you allow us to do

You allow Bookkeeper to read your company's QuickBooks data to do the work agreed in your engagement, and to write to your company only as you have approved in writing. You can withdraw this at any time by disconnecting in QuickBooks or by telling us.

3. What Bookkeeper is and is not

Bookkeeper prepares work for a named person to check and approve. It does not make professional judgements, and nothing it prepares is final until that person has approved it. You remain responsible for your business's records, decisions and filings.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Keep your Intuit sign-in details private and tell us at once if you think someone else has used them.
  • Give us records that are complete and accurate as far as you know.
  • Use Bookkeeper only for your own business and only lawfully.

5. Your data

Your data stays yours. We handle it as our privacy policy describes and only to do your work.

6. Availability

We work to keep Bookkeeper available and accurate. It depends on Intuit's service and on the internet, so it may sometimes be unavailable. We will tell you of any interruption that affects your work.

7. Liability

Our responsibility to you, and its limits, are set out in your engagement with LUNATIKS. Nothing in these terms limits any responsibility that the law does not allow to be limited.

8. Ending the connection

Either of us may end the connection at any time. When it ends we delete its key and stop reading your company. Records we must keep are kept and deleted as the privacy policy explains.

9. Law

These terms are governed by the same law, and disputes go to the same courts, as your engagement with LUNATIKS.
